BEFORE YOU SCAN

Privacy & limits

01

Your photo

A scan sends the selected image to Perfect Corp / YouCam. Skin Casefile does not save the photo. YouCam processes it under its terms and privacy policy.

02

Your record

Returned score values and the notes you type are saved in this browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to an account or backed up. Delete an individual case here or clear site data to remove everything.

03

What a score means

A photo-based score is one observation. Lighting, distance, angle, and other factors can change it. Score movement is not proof that a product caused an effect, and this app does not diagnose or recommend treatment.
