HOW IT WORKS

A measurement is more useful with a timeline.

  1. 01

    Open a case

    Name what you noticed, then add an optional note or routine change in your own words.

  2. 02

    Choose a real reading

    With your consent, a selfie is sent to YouCam Skin Analysis. Only a real completed result is saved to your case.

  3. 03

    Follow the record

    Come back with a follow-up scan. Review dates, measurements and your notes together, or print a summary to discuss with a qualified professional.

WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT

01

Context stays beside the reading

Your dates, notes and product timing travel with the measurement, so a number is not left to speak for itself.
